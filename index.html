India Young Professionals Scheme visa

Published 11 October 2026

Contents

  • Overview
  • Eligibility
  • Documents you must provide
  • Apply

Overview

An India Young Professionals Scheme visa allows Indian citizens between 18 and 30 years old to live and work in the UK for up to 2 years.

You must be selected in the India Young Professionals Scheme ballot before you can apply for this visa. To enter the ballot you must declare that you're eligible for the visa - check if you're eligible before you enter.

If you're a UK citizen, you can apply for the Young Professionals Scheme to live and work in India.

Rules for Visa

  • Must have at least £50,000 in savings.
  • Cannot have any children under the age of 18 who live with you or for whom you're financially responsible.
  • Cannot have previously lived in the UK under the scheme.
  • Effective From 20-10-2026 the new rules will come into effect.
